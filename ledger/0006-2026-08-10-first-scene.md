# Ledger 0006 — the first scene, and what the system did when it met one

```yaml
date: 2026-08-10
project: the-door
trigger: milestone (first rendered prose; META-1 discharged)

```

## What happened

Fourteen versions of methodology and no prose. `ch03.s02` is the first scene
ever rendered under NAS, written the same day the proposal era closed
(ledger 0005), from the fourteen-word seed introduced in §0.1 three commits
earlier.

The scene was written **through the ladder**, not around it: interface first,
beats second, gate third, prose last. Nothing in the frontmatter was
back-filled from the prose — which matters, because a design artifact
reconstructed after the fact proves nothing.

## The system failed three checks, and that is the result

| Check | Verdict |
|---|---|
| **PILLAR-1** | ❌ three preconditions unpaid — `reader.confidence(fact_threshold_rule)`, `trust(wren→tal) ≥ 0.5`, `obedience_established` |
| **CONTRACT-1** | ⚠ chapter declared 2 steps of stake escalation, scene delivered 1 |
| **MODAL-4** | ⚠ `fact_threshold_rule` is a high-layer `must` deriving from no world node |

**PILLAR-1's failure is the load-bearing finding.** The pillar bound to a
rendered scene whose backward obligations do not exist, and the system named
them — three specific scenes owed, each with a stated condition, derived rather
than outlined. `/Cut.md` then materialised as seven positions with six nulls,
three of them carrying a named precondition. **Nobody wrote an outline.** The
obligation list is what §5 claims pillars produce, produced.

The break was taken deliberately and cited (`PILLAR-1-EX1`, §14.6) rather than
suppressed. A first scene rendered out of order is the correct trade against
fourteen versions of nothing; recording it as an exception is what keeps the
gate meaningful for the next one.

**CONTRACT-1's failure is smaller and sharper.** The chapter contract declared
`escalate: 2` before any prose existed; the scene delivered one step. So either
`s03` is owed or the contract over-declared — and that question arrived
*mechanically*, from a fold, at a cost of zero rereading. This is §4's
test-driven chaptering doing exactly the advertised thing on its first outing.

## What the machinery actually earned

Not everything ratified was exercised. What was:

- **`held → pursued`** (§8.5) — Wren's inert valence ends at b5. The scene's
  last line is a `move`, and the arc is now a fold with one entry in it.
- **The foreclosing pair** (VAL-4) — b4 is `val_wren_standing` foreclosing
  `val_wren_justification`: she cannot ask without risking the answer, and asks
  anyway. That beat was *derived from the schema*, not invented — the pair
  existed before the scene did.
- **Two-place modality** (§7.8) — the scene's engine is canon `saw` versus
  Wren's read `must`. Her recitation is the read; "Who told her?" is the crack.
- **The field** (§7.6, OBS-2) — `at_a_threshold: "holds; does not ask why"`
  fires inside the Practice's field. The whole scene is that method under load.
- **Facet collision** (§3.4) — b3 is `facet_steady_hand` meeting the one
  audience it was never built for. The seed image *was* the collision, named
  eight decisions before the prose existed.

## What did not help

**The interface did not write the prose, and did not try.** Nothing in the
frontmatter produced the pine door, the warp along the bottom edge, the brass
thumb-latch, the quarter-inch heel slip, or the collapse on the second syllable
of her name. Those came from drafting, and the contract stayed on its own side
of the seam — which is §2.5's healthy band rather than Wall 1, and is the first
observation in this project that bears on it at all.

**Unmeasured:** whether the design phase made the prose *faster* or *better*.
One scene, one author, no control. NAS-C4 (discarding design artifacts is
cheaper than discarding prose) remains untested — nothing was discarded.

## Honest caveat

One scene is not a post-mortem. §14.7's standard stands: **NAS is "complete but
not yet definitive" until it survives a finished work.** What changed today is
narrower and still worth recording — the system produced prose, the prose
produced three failing checks, and the failing checks produced a work list
nobody had to think of.

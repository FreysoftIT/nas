## What was run

REG-1 (v0.19) says a rule no artifact has ever triggered is **dormant**, and that
dormant must be reported separately from passing. This is its first application,
run against the whole register and the whole corpus: 7 scenes, 7 chapter
contracts, 3 reader audits, 6 graph nodes, 1 pillar, the Cut, the manifest.

**The register holds 51 active rules**, one retired (`WORLD-1`), 53 table rows
including the header. Counted two independent ways — row count minus header minus
retired, and tier-value count — which agree.

*That is the third different number this quantity has had in two days.* Working
notes carried 52. Ledger 0018 "corrected" it to 50 and was also wrong. README says
50. **A hand-tracked tally of a derived quantity, wrong three times, by three
different methods, inside the register where GRAPH-2 lives** — and the second
error was committed in the same entry that minted REG-1 to prevent exactly this.
Corrected in NAS.md and README; recorded here rather than quietly fixed.

### Dormant, with no subject in the corpus (12)

Nothing to check, so nothing to worry about *yet*. `GRAPH-4` and `GRAPH-9` govern
generated views and queries, and the project has produced **zero** of either.
`GRAPH-10` is the honest case: every scene gate report states `n/a — no
projection materialized`, so the rule declares its own silence. `OBS-3` and
`TIME-3` need a retcon and a Cut reorder respectively; neither has happened.
`MODAL-3` prices breaking a `must`; no `must` has been broken. `MOD-3`, `SCENE-1`,
`GRAPH-6`, `READER-1`, `TIME-2`, `STAKE-1` likewise.

`STAKE-1` deserves a note: it is compliant *by absence* — no stake is authored
anywhere, which is what the rule demands. Green and vacuous are the same colour.

### Violated (3) — all `structural`, all found by this pass

**This is the finding that matters, and it is a tier problem, not a rule problem.**

`structural` is defined in §14.1 as *"impossible by construction (age is computed;
snapshots don't exist)"*. **20 of 51 rules — 39% of the register — carry that
tier. There is no construction.** The corpus is hand-maintained markdown. Every
structural rule is currently enforced by author discipline, frequently expressed
as a comment asserting compliance, and a tier that says *impossible* is precisely
the tier nobody thinks to check.

The three that are failing right now:

1. **GRAPH-8** — `faction_the_league` carries `level: institution` with the
   comment `# DERIVED from member_of depth`. **The node has no `member_of` of its
   own.** The depth it claims to derive from does not exist; the value is typed.
2. **GRAPH-2** — the same `level:` fields, plus `members: [char_marek, char_kes,
   char_oyo, ...]` on the faction, which is a hand-maintained inverse index of the
   `member_of` on three character nodes. It also contains a literal `...`.
3. **OBS-1** — *no fact is canon without an observation record.* The string
   `observation_record` appears in **zero files in the project.** Eighteen facts
   are referenced. None carries one.

And one historical: **SCENE-3** (structural) was violated corpus-wide for the
entire life of the project — `exit_deltas` missing from all seven scenes while
nine contracts reconciled against it — repaired in v0.18 (ledger 0017). It is now
exercised. *A structural rule was violated everywhere, for months, undetected.*
That is the empirical proof that the tier is currently aspirational.

## The finding underneath: 49 of 64 node ids have no defining artifact

Scanning every `fact_*`, `val_*`, `char_*`, `loc_*`, `setup_*`, `rv_*`, `theme_*`
id referenced anywhere in the corpus: **64 distinct ids, 15 defined, 49
undefined.**

| Namespace | Referenced | Defined |
|---|---:|---:|
| `val_*` | 8 | 8 ✅ (inside character nodes) |
| `char_*` | 6 | 3 (3 walk-ons undefined) |
| `fact_*` | 18 | **1** |
| `setup_*` | 10 | **0** |
| `loc_*` | 7 | **0** |
| `rv_*` | 15 | **0** |
| `theme_*` | 1 | **0** |

Some of this is probably fine — `rv_*` are declared inline inside
`intended_reader_trajectory`, and walk-ons may not need nodes. **But nothing in
NAS says which namespaces require a defining artifact and which are ids-in-place,
so the by-design cases and the defects are indistinguishable** — which is the same
shape as everything else in this ledger.

Two are not fine. **18 facts with 1 node** is what makes OBS-1 unverifiable rather
than merely unverified. And **10 setups with 0 nodes** is the root cause of ledger
0018: `setup_one_more` was never an object, only a string appearing in one
`setups_planted` list and one `payoffs_resolved` list. RENDER-1 fixed the symptom
— a plant that existed in metadata and not in prose. **The cause is that a setup
has no artifact to be absent from.**

## Assessment

The prediction that opened this pass was that dormancy is measurable and the
number is unknown. It is now measured: **18 dormant, 6 of them with live subjects,
3 rules actively failing, and one tier that describes a guarantee the substrate
cannot make.**

Every previous entry in this ledger found defects one at a time, by doing
something. This one found them by *asking the register about itself*, and it cost
one session with no new prose. That is the cheapest yield-per-effort so far and
it argues that REG-1 should run on a cadence rather than on inspiration.

The honest read on the tier problem: **`structural` should mean "the engine makes
this impossible," and until an engine exists the correct tier for those 20 rules
is `gate` or `lint`.** Retiering them is not proposed here — that is a 20-rule
change and it wants the user's decision, plus it may be the wrong fix if
SOFTWARE.md is close enough that the engine arrives first. Recorded as the open
question this pass produced (§15).
